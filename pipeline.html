4-STAGE ARCHITECTURAL BLUEPRINT

How Sabr Systems Turns High-Entropy Data Silos Into Governed Agentic Memory

Moving raw tables with Fivetran or dumping PDFs into a vector store is not data readiness. Our Ivy League engineering team deploys a rigorous 4-stage pipeline inside your VPC that resolves entities, compiles business meaning, enforces ACL permissions, and guarantees deterministic LLM reporting.

STAGE 01 · DATA HYGIENE & ENTITY RESOLUTION

1. Purge, Deduplicate & Parse

Before a single token is embedded, Sabr Systems runs probabilistic entity resolution and layout-aware document parsing across your entire corpus. We eliminate the "Garbage In" that causes 73% of RAG failures.

  • ✓ Cross-System Golden Records: Links Acme Corp in Salesforce, ACME_INC_09 in SAP, and #ext-acme in Slack under a single deterministic UUID.
  • ✓ Layout-Aware Table & PDF Extraction: Preserves multi-page financial tables, footnotes, and bounding-box coordinates from scanned MSAs and invoices.
  • ✓ Temporal & Supersession Hygiene: Automatically flags superseded 2023/2024 drafts so agents only retrieve currently effective policies and pricing schedules.
STAGE 02 · SEMANTIC LAYER & ONTOLOGY

2. Tagging, Taxonomy & Governed Metrics

Vector similarity alone cannot calculate Net Revenue Retention or join five ERP tables. We engineer a formal semantic layer and knowledge graph that compiles business questions into deterministic SQL + contextual prose.

  • ✓ Canonical Metric Definitions: Single version-controlled YAML specification for ARR, Gross Margin, Churn, and Inventory Days across every department.
  • ✓ Automated Metadata Enrichment: Every chunk is tagged with domain, effective_date, confidentiality_tier, and source_authority_score.
  • ✓ Entity-Relationship Knowledge Graph: Maps how suppliers, SKUs, plants, contracts, and GL accounts connect across structured and unstructured silos.
STAGE 03 · HYBRID VECTORIZATION & SECURITY

3. Context-Aware Vectorization & Zero-Leak RBAC

We replace naive fixed-size chunking with hierarchical parent-child embeddings, hybrid lexical + dense + graph retrieval, and compile-time identity enforcement synced with Okta, Azure Entra ID, or AWS IAM.

  • ✓ Hierarchical Semantic Chunking: Preserves document section headers and parent context on every child vector so meaning is never orphaned.
  • ✓ Compile-Time ACL Pre-Filtering: User permissions are evaluated before vector retrieval and SQL compilation—preventing HR or executive data leaks.
  • ✓ Event-Driven CDC Streaming: Kafka/Debezium & webhook listeners re-index modified records and revoke permissions in under 5 seconds.
STAGE 04 · AGENTIC BI & OBSERVABILITY

4. Executive LLM Reporting & Continuous Eval Harness

Once your data is tidied, we expose it through our Executive LLM Reporting Suite and standardized MCP/OpenAPI endpoints so your internal agents and leadership team get instant, cited answers.

  • ✓ Conversational Executive BI: Ask complex multi-hop questions in Slack, Teams, or Web and receive verified charts, SQL lineage, and PDF page citations.
  • ✓ CI/CD Retrieval Evaluation Suite: Every pipeline update is tested against 500+ golden question-answer pairs for precision, recall, and faithfulness.
  • ✓ Model-Agnostic Routing: Works seamlessly with Claude, OpenAI, DeepSeek, Gemini, or self-hosted Llama/Qwen models inside your VPC.
