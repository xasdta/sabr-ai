STEP 01 · CLEANING DATABASES & DOCUMENTS

1. Remove Duplicates, Errors & Outdated Drafts

Before AI touches your information, we clean up your database tables and document files so bad data never leads to a bad business decision.

  • ✓ Combine Duplicate Database Records: If the same client or vendor appears under slightly different names in different database tables, we merge them into one clean profile.
  • ✓ Accurately Read Tables Inside PDFs: Standard AI scrambles multi-page pricing and financial tables inside PDFs. We extract every row and column cleanly.
  • ✓ Separate Active Documents from Old Drafts: We tag expired contracts and old policy drafts so AI only uses your current, signed documents.
STEP 02 · CONNECTING DOCUMENTS TO DATABASES

2. Link Your Documents Directly to Your Database Numbers

Your database knows what happened (the numbers), while your documents explain why (the contract terms, policies, and agreements). We connect them together.

  • ✓ One Official Definition for Every Metric: We lock in your company’s exact formula for revenue, profit margin, retention, and costs so every report matches 100%.
  • ✓ Clear Labels on Every Document: Every PDF and document is tagged with its effective date, department, client/vendor ID, and document type.
  • ✓ Direct Database-to-Document Links: When you ask about a client or vendor, AI sees both their database transactions and their signed PDF agreements at the same time.
STEP 03 · MAKING DATA AI-SEARCHABLE & PRIVATE

3. Organize for Instant Search & Strict Privacy

We format and index your cleaned databases and documents so AI can search millions of records in a split second—without ever exposing private information to the wrong person.

  • ✓ Smart Document Organization: Instead of chopping PDFs into random fragments, we keep whole sections, headings, and tables intact so meaning is never lost.
  • ✓ Role-Based Privacy Controls: Finance, HR, and executive documents stay restricted to authorized leaders—AI automatically respects your company’s permissions.
  • ✓ Automatic Daily Sync: Whenever a new row is added to your database or a new PDF contract is uploaded, it is cleaned and indexed automatically.
STEP 04 · AI REPORTING & ASSISTANTS

4. Ask Plain-English Questions & Get Verified Reports

Once your databases and documents are organized, we give your team a clean, easy-to-use AI reporting interface—or connect directly to the AI tools you already use.

  • ✓ Plain-English Business Reporting: Ask questions like "Which contracts renew next quarter and what was their profit margin?" and get an instant chart and summary.
  • ✓ Clickable Proof on Every Answer: Every number and statement includes a direct link to the exact database record and PDF page it came from.
  • ✓ Works With Any Major AI Model: Compatible with OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, or private models running inside your own cloud.
