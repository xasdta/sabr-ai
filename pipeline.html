STAGE 01 · DATABASE FIELD MAPPING & SEMANTICS

1. Map Cryptic Columns & Disjointed Tables into Plain English

If your databases have abbreviated column names (`AMT_NET_02`, `FLG_9`), undocumented status codes, and unlinked keys across tables, we build a clear semantic dictionary so AI LLMs understand every field without guessing.

  • ✓ Column-by-Column Definitions: Translates developer shorthand into business concepts and documents every numeric status code.
  • ✓ Verified Cross-Table Joins: Maps primary/foreign keys and bridges mismatched ID formats across separate tables.
  • ✓ Official KPI Formulas: Locks in your company’s exact math for revenue, margin, churn, inventory, and utilization.
STAGE 02 · UNIFIED CROSS-DATABASE QUERY ENGINE

2. Query Live Across MySQL, Postgres & SQL Server in Place

When your data is spread across multiple database systems, we set up a distributed SQL query engine that connects directly to all of them—allowing AI agents to join tables across different databases in one live query.

  • ✓ Zero-Copy Federated Joins: Join MySQL orders with PostgreSQL subscriptions and SQL Server financials on the fly.
  • ✓ One Unified Catalog: Exposes every connected database under a single, consistent SQL interface for AI LLMs.
  • ✓ Read-Only Guardrails: Protects your source databases with strict read-only execution and query timeouts.
STAGE 03 · ULTRA-FAST COLUMNAR ANALYTICAL ENGINE

3. Transform Clean Data Into a Millisecond AI Analytical Store

If your data is already clean—or once we finish mapping it—we transform and stream it into a high-speed, columnar analytical database engine purpose-built for heavy AI LLM queries and real-time corporate automation.

  • ✓ Sub-Second Scans on 100M+ Rows: Columnar storage and vectorized execution let AI agents aggregate years of data in under 50 milliseconds.
  • ✓ 100% Offloaded from Production DBs: AI agents and employees can run thousands of complex queries without putting a single ounce of load on your live customer-facing databases.
  • ✓ Real-Time Streaming & Rollups: Continuous change-data streaming and automatic pre-computed views keep every KPI up to the second.
STAGE 04 · AI LLM ACCESS, REPORTING & AUTOMATION

4. Connect AI LLMs & Agents to Report and Automate Functions

With your data mapped and powered by our high-speed analytical engine, we connect AI LLMs and custom agents that empower your users and automate work across the corporation.

  • ✓ Self-Serve AI Data Access for Users: Executives and authorized staff ask questions in plain English and get instant charts and tables.
  • ✓ Automated Corporate Workflows: AI agents monitor your live data to trigger restock orders, flag margin anomalies, route retention alerts, and compile board packets automatically.
  • ✓ Role-Based Security & Audit Logs: Every user and AI agent only sees the rows and columns permitted for their role.
EVEN IF YOUR DATA IS ALREADY CLEAN

Why Clean Transactional Databases Still Need Our High-Speed Analytical Engine for AI LLMs

Standard app databases (like MySQL, Postgres, and SQL Server) store data row-by-row for single transactions—not for AI agents scanning millions of records at once.

✗ POINTING AI LLMs DIRECTLY AT STANDARD APP DATABASES
Row-by-Row Bottleneck: Scanning 25 million rows to calculate a 12-month trend takes 30–90 seconds per AI question—or times out completely.
Production Outage Risk: If 20 employees or background AI agents run heavy analytical queries at the same time, your live MySQL/Postgres app slows down for customers.
No Pre-Computed AI Context: Every time an AI agent checks for anomalies or runs a workflow, it has to recompute raw joins from scratch.
✓ TRANSFORMED INTO SABR’S HIGH-SPEED AI ANALYTICAL ENGINE
Millisecond Columnar Speed: Data is compressed and organized by column, allowing AI LLMs to aggregate 50M+ rows in 20 to 60 milliseconds.
Zero Load on Production Systems: Your live operational databases stay 100% isolated while hundreds of users and AI agents query the high-speed analytical store concurrently.
Powers Real-Time Corporate Automation: AI agents continuously evaluate live metrics and trigger automated workflows, alerts, and reports without lag.
