STEP 01 · DEFINING CRYPTIC DATA FIELDS

1. Translate Confusing Columns Into Plain Business Definitions

Most company databases have hundreds of tables with abbreviated column names created years ago (`AMT_NET_02`, `FLG_9`, `CD_TYPE_B`). We inspect your schemas and build a clear business dictionary for every field.

  • ✓ Column-by-Column Business Meaning: Maps cryptic field names to exact business concepts so an AI agent never confuses gross bookings with recognized net revenue.
  • ✓ Status & Category Code Lookup: Documents what every numeric code (`STATUS = 1, 2, 3, 4`) actually means in your business workflow.
  • ✓ Filter Rules for Clean Reporting: Explicitly tells the AI which internal test accounts, cancelled orders, or staging rows must be excluded from live reports.
STEP 02 · CONNECTING DISJOINTED TABLES

2. Map the Relationships Across Separate Tables & Databases

Even when individual tables have good data, they are often disjointed across different databases or modules. We map the exact join paths so an AI agent can pull across multiple tables without breaking.

  • ✓ Verified Cross-Table Join Paths: Defines the exact primary and foreign key relationships across your customer, order, inventory, and financial tables.
  • ✓ Cross-Database ID Bridges: Connects records when one database uses an integer ID (`10492`) and another table uses an account code (`ACCT-10492-US`).
  • ✓ Granularity & Date Alignment: Ensures daily transaction tables, monthly budget tables, and line-item tables join cleanly without multiplying rows.
STEP 03 · LOCKING IN BUSINESS LOGIC & METRICS

3. Define Your Company’s Official KPI Formulas

If you ask an AI agent for "Net Margin" or "Customer Churn" without defining the formula, it invents its own math. We lock your exact business formulas into a governed mapping layer.

  • ✓ Single Source of Truth for Metrics: Every department and AI agent uses the exact same formula for Revenue, Margin, Retention, Backlog, and Utilization.
  • ✓ Role-Based Field Security: Sensitive columns (like payroll, cost basis, or executive financials) are restricted by user role before any AI query runs.
  • ✓ Schema-Change Protection: If your engineering team adds or renames a database column, our mapping layer alerts and updates automatically.
STEP 04 · CONNECTING AI AGENTS & REPORTING

4. Plug Your Mapped Databases Into AI Agents

Once your tables and fields are mapped, any AI agent can understand your databases immediately—allowing executives and teams to run complex reports just by asking in plain English.

  • ✓ Conversational Executive Reporting: Ask multi-table business questions and get instant charts, variance breakdowns, and verified numbers.
  • ✓ Ready-to-Use AI Agent Endpoints: Connect your mapped database layer to internal AI assistants, automated workflows, or scheduled board reports.
  • ✓ Works With Any AI Model: Compatible with OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, or private models hosted inside your own cloud.
