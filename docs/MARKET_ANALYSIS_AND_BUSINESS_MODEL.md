## 1. Executive Summary: Why Clean Databases & Documents Are the #1 Requirement for Business AI

Between 2024 and 2026, thousands of businesses purchased AI tools expecting instant reporting and automation—only to discover that AI cannot give reliable answers when company **databases** contain duplicate records and business **documents (PDFs, contracts, invoices, SOPs)** are unorganized and disconnected from database tables.

- **70% to 80% of company AI projects fail before reaching daily use**, and **73% of wrong AI answers** are caused by messy, outdated, or unlinked data—not the AI model itself.
- **80%+ of business rules, contract terms, and pricing exceptions live inside PDF documents** that traditional databases and dashboard tools cannot read.
- **4.6x Higher Success Rate (IDC 2026 Survey):** Companies that clean and connect their databases and documents first succeed with AI 4.6x more often and achieve **3.5x stronger financial gains (+10.2% vs. +2.9%)**.
- **$31.29B Market in 2026 → $85.91B by 2031 (22.38% Annual Growth):** Global demand for AI data preparation and cleanup services is compounding at over 22% per year.
