## 1. Executive Thesis: Why "legacy brand for Enterprise Data" Wins in 2026

Between 2024 and 2026, enterprises invested heavily in foundation model licenses and internal AI agent pilots, only to hit a structural wall: **models are commoditized, but clean, governed enterprise context is scarce.**

- **70% to 80% of enterprise RAG and AI agent projects fail to reach production** (Gartner / AINative / SaaS Sentinel 2026 benchmarks), and **73%–80% of those failures originate in the data ingestion, entity resolution, chunking, and retrieval layer**—not the LLM itself.
- **80% to 90% of organizational data is unstructured** (PDF contracts, scanned invoices, Slack threads, Jira tickets, SharePoint repositories), and **only 39% of enterprises** report that their unstructured data is prepared for AI consumption.
- **Only 25% of enterprises run a governed semantic layer in production** today. Without a semantic layer that defines canonical metrics (ARR, Gross Margin, NRR, Inventory Turns) and join paths across ERP/CRM tables, raw Text-to-SQL and naive vector RAG hit an accuracy ceiling where silent hallucinations make executive reporting unusable.
- **4.6x Production Success Multiplier (IDC CDO Survey):** Organizations with mature, governed data products achieve **65% production agentic AI deployment** compared to just **14%** among low-data-maturity peers, driving **3.5x higher financial KPI gains (+10.2% vs. +2.9%)**.
