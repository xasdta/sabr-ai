Why Both Data Mapping and High-Speed Analytical Architecture Are Essential

Some companies come to us with messy, disjointed tables where AI doesn’t know what the fields mean. Other companies already have clean, well-mapped data—but their databases are built for row-by-row app transactions, so AI agents time out when trying to run company-wide reports or automations.

  • ✓ Solving the Meaning Problem (Field Mapping & Live Federation): We define every column and KPI rule in plain English and unify your MySQL, Postgres, and SQL Server databases under one catalog.
