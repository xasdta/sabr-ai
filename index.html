The 4-Stage Sabr AI Method for Enterprise Data

We don’t just hand you another dashboard or a brittle prompt wrapper. Sabr Systems re-architects your underlying data estate into a clean, governed, machine-readable substrate.

STAGE 01 · PURGE & NORMALIZE

Systematic Cleaning & Entity Resolution

We eliminate duplicate customer/vendor records, archive stale draft documents, normalize schemas across ERPs/CRMs, and resolve fuzzy cross-system identities into canonical Golden Records.

  • ✓ Probabilistic deduplication across ERP, CRM & billing
  • ✓ Layout-aware OCR for complex PDFs & tables
  • ✓ Stale policy quarantine so LLMs never cite dead docs
STAGE 02 · TAXONOMY & ONTOLOGY

Semantic Tagging & Business Meaning

Every document chunk, database column, and Slack thread is enriched with structured metadata: business domain, temporal validity, authoritative owner, and canonical metric formulas.

  • ✓ Governed YAML metric store (one definition of NRR/EBITDA)
  • ✓ Automated PII/PHI redaction & classification tags
  • ✓ Knowledge Graph linking contracts to GL transactions
STAGE 03 · HYBRID INDEXING

Context-Aware Vectorization & RBAC

We replace naive 512-token splitting with hierarchical, semantic-boundary embeddings combined with BM25 lexical search, GraphRAG, and strict document-level access control (Okta/Entra).

  • ✓ Dense + Sparse + Graph hybrid retrieval index
  • ✓ Compile-time ACL enforcement (zero permission leaks)
  • ✓ Real-time CDC streaming keeps embeddings fresh
STAGE 04 · INTELLIGENCE LAYER

Executive AI LLM Reporting & Agents

Your leadership and operations teams query the entire enterprise in plain English—receiving board-ready reports, root-cause variance analysis, and autonomous agent execution with 100% source citations.
