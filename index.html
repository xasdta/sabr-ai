THE REAL BLOCKERS INSIDE YOUR DATABASES

Why You Can’t Just Plug an AI Agent Directly Into Your Raw Databases

Your databases were built for software applications to store rows—not for AI agents to read and understand. Without a mapped definition layer, even the smartest AI gets lost in your tables.

BLOCKER 01 · CRYPTIC FIELD NAMES

Columns Aren’t Defined in Plain English

Your database tables are full of shorthand column names like VAL_ADJ_2, TYPE_CD = 9, or FLAG_X. Humans in your company know what they mean from experience, but an AI agent has to guess—and guesses wrong.

BLOCKER 02 · DISJOINTED TABLES

Tables Don’t Connect Cleanly Across Systems

Customer data sits in one table, orders sit in another, and financials sit in a third—often using different ID formats, date conventions, and granularity. When an AI agent tries to join them on the fly, it double-counts or drops records.

BLOCKER 03 · UNWRITTEN BUSINESS RULES

KPI Formulas Live in People’s Heads

How does your company calculate "Net Margin," "Active Account," or "On-Time Delivery"? Which test accounts or voided rows must be excluded? Until those rules are mapped onto your fields, AI reports will never match your leadership numbers.
