## What We Sell (Complete Service Synthesis)

**Sabr Systems** is the complete bridge between your company’s databases and working AI LLMs and agents. Whether your databases are currently disjointed and hard for AI to understand, or your data is already clean and ready for scale, we engineer the end-to-end architecture that makes your data usable by AI:

1. **Path A — If Your Databases Are Disjointed or Cryptic (Field Mapping & Live Cross-Database Querying):**
   - We map shorthand column names (`TRN_AMT_02`, `STAT_CD=4`) and status codes into plain-English business definitions, lock in your official KPI formulas, and deploy a **Unified Cross-Database Query Engine** that joins tables live across MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQL Server, Oracle, and cloud databases with zero data migration.
2. **Path B — If Your Data Is Already Clean & Mapped (Ultra-Fast AI Analytical Engine & Automation):**

We transform and stream your clean data into a **high-speed, columnar analytical database engine** where AI LLMs can aggregate tens of millions of rows in milliseconds (`<50ms`) with **zero load on your live production servers**.
3. **Turnkey AI LLMs, Self-Serve Data Access & Corporate Automation:**
   - On top of this architecture, we build and deploy custom AI agents and LLM interfaces that let executives and employees access company data in plain English, generate instant verified reports, and **automate operational functions across the corporation** (e.g. automated restock purchase orders, retention workflows, margin anomaly alerts, and scheduled board packets).
