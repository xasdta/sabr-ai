## What We Do

Companies already have valuable data sitting in their databases—but they cannot connect an AI agent to use it or report on it. Why? Because database tables are **disjointed** across systems and filled with **cryptic, poorly defined column names** (`TRN_AMT_02`, `STAT_CD = 4`, unlinked keys, unwritten KPI logic) that AI doesn't understand.

**Sabr AI by Sabr Systems** maps every cryptic database field into plain-English business definitions, connects disjointed tables across your databases, locks in your official KPI formulas, and connects your databases to AI agents so your business can run instant, accurate reports.
